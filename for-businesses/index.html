How the consulting work runs, step by step.

  1. 01
    First: we learn your business.

    How you win work, where the money leaks, what your people spend their days on, and what you have quietly stopped believing is possible. Not a workshop, not a questionnaire. We sit in the building and ask questions.

  2. 02
    Then: we map what AI opens, and where it stops.

    One page. On one side: the services you could offer, the speed you could reach, the costs you could cut. On the other: what stays with your people, because your name, your judgement or your relationships are on it. Read back to you, and you approve or reject each line before anything is built. That page is the plan.

  3. 03
    Then: we prove the first ideas live.

    In your office, with your data, in your own accounts. Two or three ideas from the page get tested. The ones that work stay. The ones that fail cost you an afternoon and teach you something.

  4. 04
    Last: your team takes over, and we decide what is next.

    The people who will run what works learn to change the rules without us, and each system gets a named owner on your side who has already changed one. Then we look at the page together and decide whether we take on the Head of AI role for the months ahead.

Everything is yours from day one.

Every system runs in your accounts, under your logins, on your infrastructure. The code sits in your repository. The API keys are yours and billed to you directly, so you can see and cap what they cost. We hold no credential you cannot revoke in an afternoon.

Every system gets a written handover.

Written the week the system is built, not the week we leave: what runs where, which service does what, how to change the rules, how to switch it off, what breaks if you do, and what to look for when something looks wrong. Written for the person on your team who will own it, and tested by having them use it while we watch.

A named person on your side owns each system.

We do not hand over to “the team”. Before anything goes live, one of your people is trained on it, has changed a rule themselves, and is named in the handover as its owner.

Response times, in writing.

Same working day for anything that is broken, two working days for everything else, and a named window each week when you know where to find us. If we are away for more than a week, you get the dates in advance.

If we stop, nothing stops.

No system needs a licence, account, server or subscription of ours. If we disappeared tomorrow, everything we built would keep running and your named owners could keep changing it. That is the test we design against, and you can put it in the contract.

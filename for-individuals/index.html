What stays with you.

  • The decision. We lay out the options and what AI can find out about each of them. You choose.
  • Your money. We are not financial, legal or medical advisers, and we say so the moment a question crosses into that ground.
  • Your data. Nothing of yours lives on anything of ours.
  • The skill. You leave able to do the next one without us, if that is what you want.

What we will not do.

  • Tell you which house to buy, which investment to make or which contract to sign. We show you how to find out more, faster, and how to check what a model tells you before you rely on it.
  • Do a child’s homework, or leave a child alone with a chatbot. We teach how it works, where it is wrong, and how to use it well — with the adult in the room.
  • Sell you a subscription, a course or a platform of ours. There is none.
  • Take on a question where AI adds nothing. You hear that in the first call, for free.

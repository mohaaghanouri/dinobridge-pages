For individuals

An AI expert on your side, for the thing in front of you.

Most of what is written about AI is written for companies. Most of the decisions it could help with are made by one person: what to do with a large sum of money, whether an idea is worth a year of your life, how to start the company you keep talking about, which of your skills to invest in next, what to tell your child about a technology that will shape their working life.

Dinobridge gives you the same expert a company gets, sized to one person. You bring the question. We bring what AI can and cannot do about it, in plain language, with no product of ours to sell you — and where it can help, we make sure you leave able to use it yourself.

Three ways to work with us.

  1. Consult

    You bring the question — a decision, a plan, an idea, the next step in your work. We bring what AI can and cannot do about it, in plain language, and a straight recommendation you can act on the same day. One session, or a few.

  2. Enable

    Your own tools, on your own accounts, set up for what you actually do — and you learn to work with them at your own pace, on your own material. That includes teaching a child how this works and where it goes wrong, at their level, with the house rules to go with it.

  3. Build

    A prototype you can put in front of a co-founder or an investor, a tool you use every day, the first version of the platform behind the company you are starting. Quoted after we have understood it, built in your accounts, and yours from the first line of code.

How it runs.

  1. You book a free thirty-minute call or write to us. No preparation; a few sentences about what you are dealing with is enough.
  2. We tell you honestly whether AI changes anything here. Roughly one time in three the answer is that it does not, and you leave with a recommendation instead of a proposal.
  3. If it does, you get a fixed price in writing before anything starts: for a session, a series of them, or a build. No meter running.
  4. We meet on video, or in person in Berlin. Everything we set up runs in your own accounts and on your own devices, and stays yours.

What stays with you.

  • The decision. We lay out the options and what AI can find out about each of them. You choose.
  • Your money. We are not financial, legal or medical advisers, and we say so the moment a question crosses into that ground.
  • Your data. Nothing of yours lives on anything of ours.
  • The skill. You leave able to do the next one without us, if that is what you want.

What we will not do.

  • Tell you which house to buy, which investment to make or which contract to sign. We show you how to find out more, faster, and how to check what a model tells you before you rely on it.
  • Do a child’s homework, or leave a child alone with a chatbot. We teach how it works, where it is wrong, and how to use it well — with the adult in the room.
  • Sell you a subscription, a course or a platform of ours. There is none.
  • Take on a question where AI adds nothing. You hear that in the first call, for free.

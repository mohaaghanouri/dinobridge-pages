For individuals

A straight answer, and the confidence to use AI yourself.

You bring a decision, an idea or a skill you want to learn. You leave with a plain answer on what AI can do about it, and able to use it yourself.

Read more

Most of what is written about AI is written for companies, yet many of the decisions it can help with are made by one person: a large sum of money, an idea worth a year of your life, a child to explain it all to. Dinobridge gives you the same senior team a company gets, sized to one person, with no product of ours to sell you.

Advice, a new skill, or a tool of your own.

  • Consult

    A straight recommendation you can act on the same day. You bring the question — a decision, a plan, an idea, the next step in your work — and we tell you plainly what AI can and cannot do about it. One session, or a few.

  • Enable

    You learn to use AI well, on your own material and at your own pace. We set up the right tools on your own accounts, for what you actually do. That can include teaching a child how it works and where it goes wrong, with house rules to match.

  • Build

    Something real to show a co-founder or an investor, a tool you use every day, or the first version of the company you are starting. A fixed price, quoted in writing after the free first call. Built in your own accounts, and yours from the first day.

What changed for people like you.

People who came without a company behind them, grouped by what they came with.

  • Job hunting

    Most of them are now in jobs found this way — people who had stopped opening the replies to their applications.

  • Sport

    Semi-professionals with a reel a scout could open the same evening, and a training log they actually kept.

  • Flat hunting

    Fewer flats, not more: a shortlist that matched the real criteria, and the documents read before the viewing rather than after.

  • Teaching

    Marking and report season cut to evenings. The tool finds the evidence; the teacher gives the grade.

  • Coaching and one-person studios

    One-person studios. For three of the five, the honest answer was a booking system, not AI, and we said so.

  • Online shopping

    Subscriptions cancelled and returns made in time paid for the fee within a month. The smallest job on this list.

Four steps, no surprises.

  1. 01
    You book a free call, or write.

    Thirty minutes, no preparation. A few sentences about what you are dealing with is enough.

  2. 02
    We tell you honestly whether AI will help.

    Roughly one time in three the answer is that it does not, and you leave with a recommendation instead of a proposal.

  3. 03
    A fixed price, quoted in writing.

    For one session, a few, or something we build for you — agreed before anything starts.

  4. 04
    We meet on video, or in Berlin.

    Everything we set up runs in your own accounts and on your own devices, and stays yours.

What stays with you.

  • The decision. We lay out the options and what AI can find out about each of them. You choose.
  • Your money. We are not financial, legal or medical advisers, and we say so the moment a question crosses into that ground.
  • Your data. Nothing of yours lives on anything of ours.
  • The skill. You leave able to do the next one without us, if that is what you want.

Honest help, with nothing to sell you.

  • You get the means to find out more, faster, and to check what AI tells you before you rely on it. Which house, which investment, which contract: that stays your call.
  • Children learn how AI works, where it is wrong and how to use it well, with an adult in the room. We do not do their homework, and no child is left alone with a chatbot.
  • Advice with nothing attached: there is no subscription, course or platform of ours to sell you.
  • If AI adds nothing to your question, you hear that in the first call, for free.

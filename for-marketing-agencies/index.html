One calm production line.

The first useful system is usually smaller than an “AI transformation” and more valuable than a collection of prompts.

  1. 01

    Collect

    Bring the brief, source files, previous work and client rules into one traceable intake.

  2. 02

    Structure

    Extract the facts, constraints, audiences, claims and missing decisions; link every fact back to its source.

  3. 03

    Prepare

    Generate research summaries, first drafts and channel variants only inside the rules the team approved.

  4. 04

    Review

    Route every material change to a named person. Nothing publishes, sends or changes budget on its own.

  5. 05

    Learn

    Keep the approved language, rejected directions and campaign evidence available for the next piece of work.

The line we do not cross.

AI can prepare the field. It cannot own the position.

  • No invented customer insight, performance claim, quotation or source.
  • No publishing, client reply or budget change without a named human approval.
  • No system that scores individual creatives or monitors activity.
  • No model trained on one client’s confidential material for another client’s work.
  • Strategy, taste, accountability and the client relationship stay with your people.

About us

What stands behind Dinobridge.

Dinobridge was founded by Moha Aghanoori, who still leads it. It is a senior team that understands your business and builds what holds up: AI systems other people depend on, that survive an audit, a handover and colleagues who never asked for the tool.

Read more

Most AI projects fail for one of two reasons. Either the person who understood the business could not build, so the thing that got built solved the wrong problem — or the person who could build did not understand the business, so it solved a real problem nobody had.

With us you get both in one team, at your table from the first call to the handover. Nothing is handed to people you have never met.

Our founder.

Why that combination is the qualification.

Engineering that holds up.

Built to the standard of software that whole companies rely on every day. It is the difference between an impressive demo and something your team can trust when nobody is watching.

Business operations.

Years of it inside organisations where the AI had to work for colleagues who never asked for it, survive a handover, and answer to an auditor. That is a different discipline from making something impressive, and it is the one that decides whether a system is still in use a year later.

Confidential work.

Work under confidentiality, where who may see what, and a full record of every step, come first rather than later. It is why sensitive work starts with what may not leave your building.

Leadership.

The people who commissioned those systems were the people accountable for the function. Explaining a trade-off to a decision-maker in their own vocabulary — and being told no, and saying no — is most of the job.

One accountable team.

The people who hear your problem are the people who build the answer, and the founder answers for every project. Nothing is handed to people you have never met.

How we work with you.

Every engagement gets a senior team and the founder’s personal attention, from the first call to the handover. That is a promise we can keep because we choose to stay small.

  • We take a small number of clients at a time. Ask in the first call whether there is room; you get a straight answer.
  • The shape of an engagement — which days, on site or on video, over how many weeks — is agreed with you in writing after the first call and before there is a contract. We will never tell you a day is available and then move it.
  • The Head of AI role is a named window every week, agreed before we start.
  • Client work and employed work are kept strictly separate. No client’s material has ever touched another engagement, and none ever will.
  • If what you need is more than that, you hear it before there is a contract. It has happened.

Your judgement stays yours.

  • Build the button that takes the decision away.
  • Automate anything where your name, your licence or your signature is on the result.
  • Sell you a system when a calendar, a form or a shared spreadsheet would fix it — that has cost us work, and it is most of the reason clients introduce us to other owners.

Every contract under control, every deadline in view, and your evenings back.

A two-partner commercial firm, an in-house team of five, three solo lawyers and a general counsel who is the entire legal function of a 300-person company. None of them is behind on the law. They are behind on everything around it: contracts that arrive, change and go missing.

A contract arrives as Vertrag_final_final_v3(2).docx. Someone replies to a version that was already out of date, and three weeks later a deadline written only in that document passes unnoticed. Nobody can say which version was signed, and the lawyer spends the evening sorting files instead of practising law.

The result.

Partner time per incoming contract has fallen from forty minutes to about eleven. Since the deadline diary began, not one notice period has been missed, and “which version is current, and what did we agree?” takes seconds instead of an afternoon. The result the partners mention first: their evenings are their own again.

What you get.

One home for every contract

Every contract the firm has handled, in one place and always in its current version, linked to its matter and to the version before it, however it arrived. The final_final_v3(2) problem is gone in the first week, and it is the change people notice first.

Every contract on one page

The key facts of each contract on a single sheet. Every fact links to the exact page and paragraph it came from, so checking it takes four seconds. A fact you cannot check is worse than none, because it looks like knowledge.

Your firm’s positions, written down

We write down, once and properly, the positions your firm actually holds. It is the hardest and most valuable week of the project, because most firms have never done it. From then on, every incoming draft is reviewed against them.

A first review of every draft

The heart of the work. Each incoming draft comes back as a Word document with tracked changes, and every suggestion carries its reason: the position it comes from, or the risk it addresses. You open it in Word, your own tool, and accept or reject each one yourself.

A deadline diary

Every date lands in your calendar, with reminders that grow more insistent as it approaches. This is where the value is highest, because a missed notice period is a claim.

Safeguards you can rely on.

Every legal judgement stays with your lawyers, and nothing leaves the firm unread.

Before you decide.

What we need from you

Two weeks of your time to write down, with us, the positions your firm actually holds rather than the ones it assumes it holds. After that, about eleven minutes per contract instead of forty, spent reading suggestions rather than searching for versions.

When we advise against it

Bespoke, high-value, one-off negotiations. Without repetition there are no settled positions to review against, and a first review of a truly novel deal would add noise rather than help.

Estate agents

Every buyer answered within minutes, and the serious ones first.

One independent Makler in Berlin, and a two-person agency doing residential sales and some rentals. They came with opposite problems: one drowning in leads he could not answer fast enough, one struggling to produce Exposés quickly enough to win the mandate.

Read more

A listing goes live and 90 enquiries arrive in 48 hours. Perhaps eight are qualified buyers. The agent answers them in the order they arrived, at 22:00, badly, and has lost the good ones on day one.

Around that: the Exposé rebuilt from scratch every time, the viewing schedule put together by hand, and the follow-ups after viewings that never get sent.

The result.

The first reply to a buyer went from hours to under five minutes, and viewing no-shows fell by more than half. Owner calls per week went down. Beyond that, you can follow qualified buyers per listing and days on market.

What you get.

The serious buyers first

Every enquiry gets a reply within minutes, with the qualifying questions attached, and the answers are sorted into qualified, incomplete and unsuitable. You then spend your attention on eight people rather than ninety. Response time decides who wins the buyer more than anything else, and it is pure routine.

Nobody good loses a slot

Qualified buyers reach your real availability without a round of messages, and the slots that get held are held by people who turn up. No-shows fell by more than half, which matters because a no-show at a block viewing costs a slot a real buyer wanted.

A complete, correct Exposé

Built from the property’s own details, with the Pflichtangaben checked — a missing Energieausweis figure is a fineable offence under GEG, and exactly the kind of thing a tired agent forgets on a Friday. The versions for the listing portals come with it.

Descriptions that tell the truth

Written from the property’s actual features, in your voice, with nothing invented. Estate agent copy has a deserved reputation and we are not adding to it: if a feature is not in the property’s details, it is not in the text.

Weekly owner update

Weekly, automatic, factual: enquiries, viewings, feedback themes. Both agents said this reduced anxious owner calls more than anything else, because most owner calls are requests for reassurance that nothing is happening without them.

Safeguards you can rely on.

The price stays your decision, and every property is shown exactly as it is.

Valuation is where your professional judgement and your reputation live, and it is the number the whole relationship turns on. The system gathers comparable listings and shows time on market. The price comes from you.

On representation: no generated images, no removing anything from a photograph, no “cosy” for tiny, no invented Baujahr, no omitted Pflichtangaben. Beyond the legal exposure, everything invented in an Exposé is discovered at the viewing — and then you are the person who wrote it. The system also never negotiates and never talks to a buyer about price.

Before you decide.

What we need from you

Answering eight good enquiries with care instead of scrolling through ninety. Filling in the property data once, properly, so the Exposé and the description have something true to work from.

When we advise against it

We advise against automated valuations and generated property photographs. We also advise against any reply that lets a buyer believe a person is writing. If a buyer is talking to a system, they should be able to tell.

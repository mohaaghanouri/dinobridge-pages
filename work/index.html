What we have actually built.

  1. A contract system for a commercial law firm

    Contracts filed and versioned the moment they arrive, every deadline in the diary, and each incoming draft opening in Word with tracked changes where every suggestion carries its reason. There is no accept-all button — we were asked for one and refused.

  2. A production contract-lifecycle platform for a corporate legal team

    Conversational intake, an in-app agentic copilot, and a Microsoft Word add-in producing native tracked-change redlines. Every AI action runs as the signed-in user, so it inherits the same permissions and the same audit trail as a human request, behind a confirm gate that pauses every change for approval. It replaced a commercial vendor costing about €70,000 a year. Built by our owner as an in-house AI engineer — the work that taught us what survives an audit.

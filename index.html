About Dinobridge

A senior team, led by its founder.

Dinobridge is a Berlin company, founded and led by Moha Aghanoori. It gives companies, teams and research groups of any size, and individuals with a decision to make, what most of them cannot hire: a senior AI team that understands the business, builds the solution and takes responsibility for it.

Read more

Moha Aghanoori founded Dinobridge and leads it. Years of professional engineering sit behind the company, spent building AI systems in production — the kind other people depend on, that survive an audit, a handover and colleagues who never asked for the tool. That experience is what the Dinobridge team brings to every client: practical advice in plain language, tools built for your business rather than for the technology, and a founder who answers for every project from the first call to the handover.

Most AI consultants either sell you a platform or automate your paperwork. We do something else. We look at your situation the way a new managing director would in their first month, ask what AI makes possible for you that was not possible a year ago, and test it with you until we know. Most people who can build with AI do not understand a business, and most people who understand a business cannot build. We do both, and we tell you plainly when AI will not pay off for you.

About us

Three promises.

Every decision stays with you.

AI prepares, drafts, sorts and proposes. The decision, the diagnosis, the grade, the valuation and the verdict stay yours. We refuse to build the button that would take them away, and we have turned down work over it.

We say no out loud.

If there is too little of the work, if every case is different, or if a calendar would fix it, you hear that instead of a proposal. It has cost us projects, and it is most of the reason clients introduce us to other owners.

Nothing depends on us that your team cannot run.

Everything runs in your own accounts, under your own logins, with your own keys. Each system gets a written handover and a named owner on your team who has already changed a rule in it before it goes live. Nothing needs a licence, server or subscription of ours. If we stay on as your Head of AI, it is because there is new ground to take, never because a tool would stop without us.

Common questions.

What happens after we get in touch?

A free thirty-minute call, with no preparation on your side. You describe your business, and we tell you honestly where AI would change something for you and where it would not. If it makes sense, you receive a written proposal at a fixed price; if not, you leave with a clear recommendation, which happens about one time in three.

Is our data safe with you?

Yes, and for regulated work it is the first thing we design. Everything runs in your own accounts, under your own logins, and you can withdraw our access in an afternoon. Before we see anything, we sign an NDA, a data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR and a one-page scope of access — and for law firms, a confidentiality undertaking under § 43e BRAO. Your content is never used for training, and the security page sets out the rest.

Who actually does the work?

A senior Dinobridge team, led by its founder, Moha Aghanoori. The people on your first call are the people who work with you, set up the tools and train your team; nothing is passed to people you have never met. Behind that are years of building AI systems that other people depend on.

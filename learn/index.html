Learn

Building with LLMs, in four lessons.

A free learning path for engineers. Four video lessons follow one example — Shopbot, a small online shop’s support assistant — from how a model picks its next word to the system that keeps it running in production.

Read more

Each lesson stands on its own and ends with practice questions: a question on screen, fifteen seconds to answer it out loud, then a strong answer. Watch them in order if the field is new to you, or go straight to the one you need.

Who it is for.

  • Software engineers starting to build with LLMs, who want the mechanism, not just the vocabulary.
  • Engineers already shipping LLM features, who want to measure them, make them safe and run them properly.
  • Anyone who has to explain these systems out loud: in a design review, to their team, or to a client.
